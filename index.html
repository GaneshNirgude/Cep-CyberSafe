TOPIC 01

Password Hygiene

Good password habits reduce the risk of account takeover and unauthorized access.

01

Use long, unique passwords

Create a different password for every important account. Longer passwords are generally harder to guess than short ones.

02

Use a password manager

A password manager can generate and securely store strong passwords so you do not have to remember every password yourself.

03

Turn on two-factor authentication

2FA adds another verification step, such as an authenticator app or security key, beyond the password.

04

Never share your passwords

Do not send passwords through chat, email, social media, or phone calls. Legitimate services should not ask you to reveal your password.

QUICK CHECKLIST

Are your password habits safe?

  • □ I use unique passwords for important accounts.
  • □ I use two-factor authentication where available.
  • □ I avoid predictable information such as my name or birthday.
  • □ I do not share passwords with other people.

TOPIC 02

Personal Data Protection

Think carefully about what information you share, where you share it, and who can access it.

01

Know what counts as personal data

Personal data can include your full name, phone number, address, email, photographs, location information, identification details, and account data.

02

Share only what is necessary

Before filling a form or posting online, ask whether the information is really needed. Avoid oversharing sensitive details publicly.

03

Check links and websites

Be careful with unexpected links, login pages, QR codes, and messages asking for personal information. Verify the source before submitting anything.

04

Review privacy settings

Regularly check privacy and app permissions on social media, browsers, phones, and other services. Remove access you no longer need.

QUICK CHECKLIST

Before sharing personal information

  • □ I know why the information is being requested.
  • □ I have checked that the website or person is trustworthy.
  • □ I avoid posting sensitive information publicly.
  • □ I regularly review app permissions and privacy settings.
